TOSHIHISA URAHIGASHI
Abstract. Step (v) of the proof of Theorem 1.10 in the fourth paper of Mochizuki’s series on
inter-universal Teichm¨ uller theory estimates, prime by prime, the log-volume of the holomorphic
hull of the union of possible images of a Θ-pilot object defined in Corollary 3.12 of the third
paper. For each tuple of places it bounds the log-volume of the component of this hull by that
of an explicit module, which depends on the order of the q-parameter at the place carrying the
label j. We give an explicit initial Θ-datum, with two places of multiplicative reduction above
the prime 30965771, and an element of the projection of the union to one summand, obtained
from a theta value by a permutation of labels allowed by the indeterminacy (Ind1), that does
not lie in this module; the corresponding component of the hull has larger log-volume than the
bound obtained in Step (v). No assertion is made here about Corollary 3.12 itself, the final
global inequality of Theorem 1.10, or the abc conjecture.

the whole packet [IUT3, Rem. 3.9.5(i)], of log-volume + ∞. Otherwise the hull is η · O for an
element η with nonzero components [IUT3, ibid.], so pr v(U 3) is an ( RI)∼-module containing
ψ(y), hence p2 · (RI)∼, and its log-volume is at least µlog(p2 · (RI)∼) = −2 logp. □
Thus, for the union of possible images and its holomorphic hull as defined in [IUT3, Cor. 3.12],
the bound that Step (v) obtains for the component at the tuple v does not hold. It is noted in
[DH, § 6.2, footnote 7] that (Ind1) contains permutations between different tensor factors, which
the automorphisms used there to estimate the hull do not include.
The example above concerns the local containment and the local upper bound used in Step (v)
for a single component. It does not by itself decide the global inequality obtained later in
Theorem 1.10 after summing over all places. No assertion is made here concerning Corollary 3.12
itself or the abc conjecture.
