## Type/action distinction and conclusion

The function side consists of multiplicative elements/functions (locally, units such as `O_vˣ`, or `K_vˣ` before restricting to norm-one units); multiplying a function by `u ∈ O_vˣ` is a value-level operation. The cyclotomic side is the inverse-system coefficient object `Λ = lim μ_n`, or finite coefficients `μ_n`, with the Galois/cyclotomic-character action and induced power automorphisms. An automorphism of `μ_n`/`Λ` is not by itself a map on the function-unit group. To apply the same exponent to theta values and q anchors, a source must supply an actual compatible action/map on the function units and a compatibility square relating it to the coefficient-module action/Kummer map. The inspected Lean declarations do not supply that joint action.

The available exact relation `q_v^{j² ℓ} = thetaRatio^{-1}` does give two anchors related by an ℓ-power equation, and profile roots differ by ℓ-torsion factors, so their local absolute values/valuations agree when defined. This is a useful **candidate** for a same-integer-valuation comparison of anchors. It does not identify distinct theaters/places, transport their valuation maps, or prove a shared cyclotomic power acts on both anchors and theta values. In particular, equal valuation of two units does not follow merely from an automorphism of the `μ_n` coefficient module.

The inspected files therefore contain nearby ingredients, but no sourced theorem that constructs the requested simultaneous q-pilot/cyclotomic synchronization. Any use of the `MonoThetaKummer` fields as if they were derived constructions, or of cyclotomic coefficient automorphisms as unit/function actions, would overstate the source. This is only a source audit and makes no claim about IUT's original mathematical status.
