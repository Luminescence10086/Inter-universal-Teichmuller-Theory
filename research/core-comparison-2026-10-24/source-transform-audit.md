## Comparison boundary

The sources contain real identities capable of relating the *values* of theta functions and real theorems specializing one actual germ at a time. They do not, in the inspected files, state a transformation identity between two full `thetaGermUnit` power series at two different parameters, nor a theorem equating their reduced Kummer classes on one identified decomposition group. The `thetaGerm` file defines its germ by coefficients at one `u` (`:152-165`) and proves its evaluation property (`:197-218`); the value transformation formulas live in `ThetaValues` / `KummerClass` and are formulas in `Kˣ` or `H¹_cont(Gal(Ω/K), μ_n)`.

If two genuine germ specialization theorems are separately instantiated, each produces a class in its own `H¹_cont(D_{A_u}, μ_n(κ(A_u))` target. To compare those classes, one must provide actual identifications/transport of the decomposition groups, residue coefficient modules, and restriction maps. Once such maps are aligned, the point-value formulas above give an exact correction by the Kummer class of the displayed constant factor. Equality follows only if that correction class is separately shown trivial (for example, the factor is an `n`th power); none of the listed source declarations makes that general claim. This avoids treating source-class equality as an assumed input.

## `Wall.lean` and finite bounds

`Iut/EtaleTheta/Wall.lean:44-45` defines `lgpValue D v j = D.qroot v ^ j ^ 2`. Its valuation-relevant declarations at `:62-90` are only the `ℓ`th-power equation and the `μ_ℓ` root ambiguity; Wall itself contains no valuation, valuation-exponent, or finite-bound theorem for `thetaRatio` or `lgpValue`.

There is a separate real norm estimate: `Iut/ThetaLink/Gauss.lean:245-258` proves `0 < ‖qroot‖` and, at bad places, `‖qroot‖ < 1`, from `qroot^{2ℓ}=q`. Also `Iut/Concrete/ThetaLocalConstruct/Data.lean:261-277` proves the bad places over `VBad` are finite. For the actual wall insertion, `Wall.lean:92-101` restricts labels to `i : Fin n` and uses `j=i.1+1`; for fixed `D` and fixed bad place, the corresponding list of `lgpValue` elements is finite. Thus, after choosing an integer-valued valuation on that completion, a finite-set maximum of the finitely many valuation differences exists. This is an independent finite-set argument for this bounded label list, not a bound stated by `Wall.lean`; it supplies no uniform bound as `D`, the place, or the label range varies.

For a more explicit relation, `Iut/EtaleTheta/ThetaValues.lean:82-84` proves `thetaRatio = s^{-j²}` when `s²=q`. Consequently an additive valuation would give `v(thetaRatio) = -j² v(s)`, and `lgpValue=qroot^{j²}` gives `v(lgpValue)=j²v(qroot)`. These are algebraic formulas from which a bound can be computed once the valuation of the chosen local root and a finite label range are fixed. The inspected Wall declarations do not provide that numeric input or an explicit integer bound for a comparison of arbitrary reduced Kummer values.

This audit identifies candidate source identities and the remaining type-alignment/bound inputs only. It makes no claim that the original IUT comparison has been established or that an IUT gap is resolved.
