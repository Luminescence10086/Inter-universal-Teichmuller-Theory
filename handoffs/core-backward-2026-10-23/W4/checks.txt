## Design, in one paragraph

`PresentationChain k` (`k ≥ 1`, `NeZero k`; `base = ⟨0, _⟩`) carries `input : Fin k → ℚ_[3]` with
`‖input i - 1‖ < 1`, transitions `step i j h` for every pair `i ≤ j` that are *functions* on `ℚ_[3]`
(`step_eq : step i j h y = y * unit i j h`, `stepInv` the inverse, `stepInv_step` the isomorphism law),
`unit_mem` (units are principal units), `unit_refl` (the identity transition multiplies by `1`), and
`step_input` (the **elementary** transition `i → i+1` really carries `input i` to `input (i+1)`).
`IsCompatible c` is the **cocycle law** `unit i l = unit i j * unit j l` for `i ≤ j ≤ l`, a hypothesis,
not a field. The reading at `i` is `localLog` of `input i` transported back to the base frame by the
inverse of the recorded transition `base → i` (`relativeInput`); it is a genuine limit
(`reading_hasSum`, `reading_is_limit_of_algorithm`, and `relativeInput_mem` keeps it in the domain).
`input_transport` (induction, cocycle used once per step) gives `input i = input base * unit base i`;
`reading_eq_base` and `reading_compatible` then give that all readings agree.

## Anti-degeneracy witness (explicit numbers)

`chainCompat` and `chainBroken`: three presentations `4, 16, 64 = 4^1, 4^2, 4^3`; units `4 ^ (j - i)`
versus `4 ^ (j - i + (j - i) / 2)`, i.e. `4` for every elementary step in both, and `4^2 = 16` versus
`4^3 = 64` for the comparison transition `0 → 2`.

* `chainCompat_isCompatible`, `chainCompat_reading` — all readings are `localLog 4`.
* `chainBroken_not_compatible` — the cocycle at `(0,1,2)` would need `64 = 16`.
* `chainBroken_reading_zero = localLog 4`, `chainBroken_reading_two = 0` (`= localLog 1`), hence
  `chainBroken_readings_differ : chainBroken.reading i0 ≠ chainBroken.reading i2` (via
  `localLog_four_ne_zero`).
* `difference_lies_in_the_direct_transition` — same presentations, same elementary units, different
  comparison unit `0 → 2`: the disagreement is caused by the transition data alone.
* `exists_incompatible_chain_with_differing_readings` — the existential package.
