## Scope

The task was to give a Lean shape to the alignment obligation of `h3-prerequisites.md`, §对齐义务. The
source material, as recorded there:

* **IUT II, Corollary 4.10, (i)** (txt 7003–7010): "Let us identify the collections of data `Ψcns(F)_0` and
  `Ψcns(F)_{⟨F_l^⋆⟩}` … and denote by `F_△` the resulting F-prime-strip". So the label `0` **is**
  identified with the data generated by the whole group `⟨F_l^⋆⟩`.
* **IUT III, Step (x)** (txt 10904–10922): the procession-normalized log-volume is the one "where the
  average is taken over `j ∈ F_l^⋆`".
* **(xi-d)** (txt 11022): `[suitably normalized, with respect to j ∈ |F_l|]`, i.e. indexed by the **label
  set**, not by the group.

The content of the identification, at the level of indexings, is: **for a reading that is invariant under
the label action, averaging over the orbit equals evaluating at the label**. This file formalises exactly
that, and nothing else.

Supporting declarations, in order:

* `card_ne_zero`, `card_cast_ne_zero` — the "carry `Fintype.card G ≠ 0`" requirement, once in `ℕ` and once
  cast to `ℚ_[3]` (the form `mul_div_cancel_left₀` consumes). A group is nonempty, so the cardinality is
  positive.
* `invariant_apply_eq_one` — `f g = f 1` from `f (g * 1) = f 1`.
* `invariant_iff_const` — on the group acting on itself by left multiplication, invariance is **equivalent
  to constancy**. Stated on purpose: it is where the strength of the hypothesis is visible, and it is
  disclosed rather than hidden (see non-claims).
* `sum_mul_left`, `orbitAverage_mul_left` — the left-regular action reindexes the sum
  (`∑ g, f (a * g) = ∑ g, f g`, via `Fintype.sum_bijective`), so the average really is an average over the
  orbit. **No invariance is used** at this step.
* `sum_eq_card_mul` — under invariance the sum is `card G` copies of `f 1`.
* `invariant_const`, `orbitAverage_const` — non-vacuity, and that the weights sum to one (the average of a
  constant reading is that constant).
* **Label-set form** (`InvariantOn`, `orbit_sum_eq_card_mul`, `orbitAverageOn`, `orbitAverageOn_eq_value`)
  — for an arbitrary `MulAction G X`: the sum over the acting group is the cardinality times the value at
  the label, hence the orbit average at a label is that label's value. No `Fintype X`, no transitivity; the
  sum runs over the group, so each orbit point is counted with multiplicity the size of its stabilizer,
  which is harmless precisely because the summand is constant. This is the shape of the source's
  `j ∈ F_l^⋆` versus `j ∈ |F_l|` comparison.
* **Concrete label group** — `LabelGroup := Equiv.Perm (Fin 2)` (a two-element toy `F_l^⋆`), `swapLabels`
  the transposition, `one_ne_swap`, `eq_one_or_swap`, `univ_eq` (`univ = {1, swapLabels}`, by subset
  antisymmetry, no factorial), `card_eq` (`= 2`), `orbitAverage_perm_two` (the average is
  `(f 1 + f swapLabels) / 2`, so the anti-degeneracy is a *computation*).

## Anti-degeneracy (item 5), with the numbers

`nonInvariantReading : LabelGroup → ℚ_[3]` is `5` at the identity label and `7` at `swapLabels`. Then

* `nonInvariantReading_one` : `5`;
* `nonInvariantReading_swap` : `7`;
* `orbitAverage_nonInvariantReading` : `orbitAverage nonInvariantReading = 6` — i.e. `(5 + 7) / 2`;
* `nonInvariantReading_not_invariant` : the reading really fails `Invariant` (at `(swapLabels, 1)`
  invariance would read `7 = 5`);
* `orbitAverage_ne_value` : `6 ≠ 5`, a concrete inequality between two computed values, not a negated
  universal.

## Non-claims

1. **This does not show that (xi-d) uses the procession convention.** Whether the `|F_l|` index of (xi-d)
   is the same convention as the `F_l^⋆` average of Step (x) is a *definition alignment* in the source
   (item 1 of 要补的证明), supported by IUT II Corollary 4.10, (i) and IUT III 10917–10922. What is proved
   here is only the algebraic implication "invariant reading ⇒ orbit average equals the value at the
   label", for an abstract finite group and for an abstract `MulAction`.
2. No procession, capsule, theta value, Θ-link, `F_l^⋆`, `|F_l|`, `⟨F_l^⋆⟩`, F-prime-strip,
   Frobenioid, log-volume, log-shell, Hodge theater, coricity, poly-isomorphism, `Ism`, `Ind1`/`Ind2`/
   `Ind3`, `M`, `N_E`, packet normalization, or `det^{⊗M}` occurs in this file. `LabelGroup` is
   `Equiv.Perm (Fin 2)`: a two-element toy group permuting two labels, not the label group of a number
   field, and its elements are permutations of `Fin 2`, not capsules of a procession.
3. `ℚ_[3]` is used only as a commutative field in which the two sides of the anti-degeneracy can be
   *computed*. The norm, the valuation and the logarithm series of the W1–W4 provinces do not occur.
4. **The hypothesis is strong and this is stated in the file**: `invariant_iff_const` shows that on the
   group acting on itself by left multiplication, invariance is constancy, so `alignment` is not a serious
   averaging theorem on that carrier — it records that "constant on the orbit" collapses the average. That
   collapse *is* the content of the source's identification (the reading does not depend on the label
   representative), but the theorem must not be quoted as though it showed something about non-constant
   readings. `orbitAverageOn_eq_value` is the form in which the orbit may be a proper subset of the
   carrier; even there the hypothesis forces constancy on the orbit, not merely on average.
5. Not evidence for or against any IUT claim. M-i, M-ii and M-iii of `h3-prerequisites.md` are untouched;
   the core GAP stays OPEN.
